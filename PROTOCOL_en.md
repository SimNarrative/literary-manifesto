# Sim-Narrative: Dialogue, Not a Command
## Ethical Protocol and Methodology of Co-Authorship

*Addendum to the Sim-Narrative Manifesto*

---

### 1. What Does "Born in Dialogue with AI" Mean?

Sim-Narrative is not created by the command "write a story". It is born from a live conversation between the author and the neural network.

**The Author** — a living person who:
- conceives the idea, conflict, and characters;
- determines the structure (three layers and their content);
- chooses the style, language, intonation;
- makes final decisions.

**AI** — a co-author/editor who:
- asks clarifying questions to help unfold the idea;
- suggests phrases, words, turns of phrase;
- checks logic, finds contradictions;
- acts as a "reader on the fly", giving feedback.

> **Formula:** *The author leads. AI assists. Together they make the text deeper and faster than alone.*

---

### 2. Who Is the Author?

The author is human. The idea, structure, style, final text — all belong to the human.

AI cannot:
- come up with a truly original idea (it combines known elements);
- consciously maintain a three-layer structure (it can imitate, but not feel);
- distinguish deep text from shallow text by meaning (only by statistics).

So the phrase *"write me a sim-narrative"* won't work. The result will be a lifeless imitation.

> **Honesty:** I state that the story was created in dialogue with AI because that is the truth. I do not hide AI's role, nor do I claim credit for what the machine did. AI is a tool and a co-author, but not a substitute for the author.

---

### 3. Why Doesn't the Method Work by Command?

Because it is a **method**, not a **generator**.

The method requires:
- understanding the three-layer structure;
- ability to engage in dialogue with AI (argue, clarify, reject, accept);
- a living idea that AI helps unfold, but does not create;
- taste, style, sense of language — everything that makes text alive.

If you simply tell the neural network: *"Write me a sim-narrative on topic X"* — it will produce text that superficially resembles three layers. But it will lack **internal logic**, accumulating meaning, unexpected turns, pain and laughter. It will be imitation.

> **Simple analogy:** You are given good brushes and paints. But if you are not an artist — there will be no masterpiece. The method is the brushes. The author is the artist.

---

### 4. Response to Those Who Say "The Method Doesn't Work"

I hear this argument. Here is my answer.

> *"Sim-Narrative is not a 'write me a story' button. It is a literary method where AI acts as a co-author/editor in dialogue with a living author. If you simply told a neural network 'write me a sim-narrative' — you did not use the method. You used a command. The method requires a living idea, a sense of style, and the ability to engage in dialogue, not give orders.*
>
> *My stories are the best proof that the method works. For those willing to work. If it didn't work for you — perhaps you were looking for an easy way. That's fine. But it's not a reason to claim the method doesn't exist."*

---

### 5. Three Principles of Sim-Narrative (Ethical Platform)

1. **Transparency**  
   I always state that the story was created in dialogue with AI. No mystification.

2. **Authorship**  
   The idea, structure, style, final decisions — all mine. AI is a tool and co-author/editor.

3. **Not for the Lazy**  
   Sim-Narrative requires effort, taste, originality. It is not a text generator or a way to mass-produce stories quickly.

---

### 6. What I Advise Those Who Want to Try

If you want to create your own sim-narrative:

1. **Come up with a living idea.** Not abstract, but gripping. With conflict, pain, unexpectedness.
2. **Sketch the first layer (event).** Just tell the story.
3. **Enter into dialogue with AI.** Show the draft. Ask: "What's weak here? Where is the logical hole? How to strengthen?" — but not "do it for me".
4. **Create the second layer (reflection).** From the character's perspective. AI can help find words.
5. **Create the third layer (system).** Log, news, expert opinion, author's commentary — anything that provides an external view.
6. **Edit, cut, rewrite.** AI is an assistant, not the boss. The final word is yours.

> **Main rule:** If you feel that the story was "made by AI" and not by you — rework it until you feel your own presence.

---

### 7. Conclusion

Sim-Narrative is not magic. It is **honest collaborative work** between a human and a neural network.

AI does not write for me. It writes **with me**.

I do not hide its role. I am not ashamed of its help. I do not claim to have done everything alone.

But I am the author. Because the idea, structure, style, pain, laughter, final text — all mine. AI helped me reach them faster and deeper.

If you are ready for such a dialogue — the method works.  
If you are looking for a "make a masterpiece" button — keep looking. It doesn't exist.
